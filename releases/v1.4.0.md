# v1.4.0 · 安卓独立每日任务 / 大世界判定 / 黑帧与断线弹窗处理 / 索拉指南定位

## 中文

### 新增安卓独立每日任务

- 新增 `src/task/AndroidDailyTask.py`，可在安卓设备（MuMu 模拟器）上直接运行每日任务，与云端/PC 端任务并列。
- `config.py` 注册该任务，`restore.py` 同步纳入清单。

### 根因修复：安卓每日任务「卡在大世界不动」

用户反馈："mumu链接有问题，日常专属任务进入大世界以后一直卡在那不动，也不跳过也没其他操作也没额外日志输出"。

**根因**：真正的断点在 `in_team()`。PC 端用右上角队伍栏的 `1/2/3` 数字徽章（`char_1_text`/`char_2_text`/`char_3_text`）判定"在队伍中"，但**安卓客户端的队伍栏是圆形角色头像，没有数字徽章**。复刻 `FeatureSet.find_one_feature` 的区域约束匹配后实测：

| 模板 | 安卓大世界实测置信度 | 阈值 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `char_1_text` | 0.150 | 0.8 |
| `char_2_text` | 0.115 | 0.8 |
| `char_3_text` | 0.129 | 0.8 |

三个模板全部落空 → `in_team()` 恒 False → `in_team_and_world()` 恒 False → `is_main()` 恒 False → `ensure_main()` 必然 600 秒超时（与日志中 `wait main` 恰好 601 秒吻合）。同时确认**没有任何现成模板可复用**：对 301 个模板做区域约束扫描与全图扫描，安卓大世界最佳匹配仅 0.615，因此判定必须走代码判据。

**新增 `src/task/android_world.py`（安卓专用大世界判定）**——用与角色/场景无关的 HUD 元素判定，阈值全部来自真实帧标定：

| 判据 | 大世界实测 | 加载界面 | 活动页 | 登录页 | 全黑帧 | 阈值 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `radar_yellow`（左上雷达黄色玩家箭头） | 339 / 347 | 0 | 29 | 0 | 0 | ≥120 |
| `ult_orange`（底部偏左协奏金球） | 534 / 717 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | ≥300 |
| `party_white`（右上队伍栏白条） | 815 / 1057 | 215 | 619 | 208 | 0 | ≥600 |
| `hp_bar_white`（底部共鸣者血条） | 3023 / 2877 | 2240 | 0 | 1073 | 0 | ≥2200 |
| `loading_bar`（底部最长连续白像素） | 93 / 94 | **1313** | 381 | 74 | 0 | ≥800 判为加载中 |

判定规则：全黑帧直接判否并单独告警；`loading_bar ≥ 800` 判否（加载界面）；`radar_yellow ≥ 120` 或 `ult_orange ≥ 300` 判真；兜底为 `party_white ≥ 600` **且** `hp_bar_white ≥ 2200` 同时成立。

> **踩过的坑**：最初只按"白像素总数"判血条，结果**加载界面被误判成大世界**（加载进度条 2240 ≥ 2200）。逐行分析后发现加载进度条是 **y=1021~1027 单行、贯穿全屏（连续 1299 px）** 的细条，而大世界血条是 **两段 85 px 的短条**（中间有刻度分隔）。改用"单行最长连续白像素长度"后两者彻底分开（1313 vs 93），阈值 800 落在活动页 381 与加载页 1313 之间，两侧都有 2 倍以上余量。

**`src/task/BaseWWTask.py` 改动**：`in_team()` 开头加 `if self.is_adb(): return self.in_team_android()`，PC 分支逻辑一行未改；新增 `in_team_android()` 返回同构三元组；PC 分支三个模板全落空时原先完全静默，现在打印各模板实际置信度。

### MuMu 抓屏黑帧拦截（`ok/device/capture_methods/nemu_ipc.py`）

- 背景：MuMu IPC 通道偶尔"返回成功但画面全黑"（实测 `mean=0`，`max=0`，非零像素占比 0）。上层拿到黑帧会认为画面正常，于是模板全不命中，表现和"不在大世界"一模一样。
- 处理：全黑帧先**重建 IPC 连接重试 3 次**（每次间隔 0.5 秒），仍失败抛带明确中文原因的 `CaptureException`；底层 `@retry` 耗尽后抛的是不带 message 的裸 `Exception`，这里补上"请确认模拟器正在运行、窗口可见、未开启后台保活"等可读原因。
- 判定阈值：整帧 `max ≤ 8` 视为空画面（正常画面即使只有一个非黑像素也不会误判）。

### 新增安卓端「连接已断开」弹窗处理

- 场景：MuMu 挂机过久或网络抖动，游戏弹【连接已断开】对话框。此时**画面背后仍然是大世界**，`in_team` 判定依然为 True，任务以为一切正常，但所有点击都被弹窗挡住——与用户反馈的"卡住不动"完全一致。
- 处理（`BaseWWTask.handle_android_disconnect()`，在 `is_main()` 里**先于**大世界判定执行）：
  1. 廉价亮度预筛：中央区域亮像素（灰度 > 200）占比 ≥ 0.5 才继续（实测弹窗 0.777，其余画面 ≤ 0.101），避免每次 `is_main` 都跑 OCR；
  2. OCR 确认文案命中 `连接已断开|断开连接|重新登录`（实测置信度 1.0）；
  3. 点掉弹窗底部的「确认」，复位 `logged_in = False`，交给既有 `wait_login` 重新登录。
- PC / 云游戏完全不受影响（`is_adb()` 为假直接返回）。

### MuMu 桥接模式 ADB 自动发现

- MuMu 桥接模式的 ADB 端口不固定，且常不在默认端口列表里；新增 ARP 扫描回退机制自动发现设备（`ok/device/DeviceManager.py`）。
- ARP 输出按 GBK 区域设置解码（`errors=replace`），避免中文 Windows 下乱码报错。

### 安卓端 F1/F2 改为直接点右上角图标

- **需求**：用户反馈"F2 打开任务环节也有问题，任务在右上角图标里面"，并指出**该图标左边那个是活动**，要求把 F2、F1 都改成在安卓版脚本里直接点击，不走按键。
- **触屏端没有 F1/F2**：PC 端 `F2` 打开「索拉指南」、`F1` 打开「活动」；安卓客户端触屏上没有这两个键，实测发 `KEYCODE_F2` 游戏无任何反应。原 `openF2Book` 的分支是 `if self.in_team_and_world() and self.touch.open_book(): ... else: send_key('f2')`——安卓端一旦因为任何原因没走进点图标分支，就会掉进发键分支，然后白等 `wait_book` 超时，最后抛"can't find gray_book_boss, make sure f2 is the hotkey for book"。

**新增 `src/task/android_book.py`（安卓索拉指南类别图标定位）**：

安卓索拉指南是移动端布局（左侧一列竖排类别图标 + 右侧列表），类别图标与 PC 模板尺寸/位置都不同。**直接拿 PC 模板做 NCC 会命中背景空白**——实测在安卓书界面上：

| 模板 | NCC 最高分 | 命中位置 | 实际图标位置 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gray_book_quest` | 0.712 | (48,156) | y≈191 |
| `gray_book_boss` | 0.741 | (44,283) | y≈322 |
| `gray_book_weekly` | 0.767 | (42,426) | y≈461 |
| `gray_book_all_monsters` | 0.822 | (39,695) | y≈727 |

最高分全部落在**模板自身锚点处的空背景**上而不是图标上——定位失败，点下去也是错位置。

改用「**白色笔画二值掩码 + 多尺度 IoU 滑窗**」：屏幕左侧图标列窄条二值化成笔画掩码 → PC 模板同样二值化并裁到笔画外接框 → 在 0.40~1.10 共 8 个缩放比例下用积分图算**逐位置精确 IoU**，取全局最高分。

关键坑位：
- **纵向下界必须卡到屏高 73%**。屏幕下方有背景渐变，会变亮成一片白；二值化后整块全 1，任何模板都能在上面拿到 **IoU=1.0** 的假匹配（实测 `gray_book_all_monsters` 命中 y=844/1038）。
- **纵向上界要卡掉标题栏**（屏高 12% 以上），否则标题的"素材获取"图标会被当成类别图标。
- 模板与屏幕要用**不同口径**的掩码：屏幕侧用笔画掩码（`S<110 且 V>150`），模板侧同时试灰度掩码与笔画掩码，取分高者。

| `gray_book_weekly` | 周常（循环徽章） | (76,461) | 0.88 |
| `gray_book_all_monsters` | 全部怪物（骷髅面具） | (80,727) | 0.90 |

### New standalone Android daily task

- New `src/task/AndroidDailyTask.py` runs the daily task directly on an Android device (MuMu emulator), alongside the existing cloud/PC tasks.
- Registered in `config.py` and added to the `restore.py` manifest.

### Root-cause fix: the Android daily task "stuck in the open world"

User report: "mumu连接有问题，日常专属任务进入大世界以后一直卡在那不动，也不跳过也没其他操作也没额外日志输出" (the MuMu connection is broken, the daily task enters the open world and then just sits there, no skip, no other action, no extra log output).

**Root cause**: the real breakpoint is `in_team()`. On PC, "in a party" is decided by the `1/2/3` numeric badges on the top-right party bar (`char_1_text`/`char_2_text`/`char_3_text`), but **the Android client's party bar shows circular character portraits with no numeric badges**. Re-running the region-constrained matching of `FeatureSet.find_one_feature` on real frames gives:

| Template | Measured confidence in the Android open world | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `char_1_text` | 0.150 | 0.8 |
| `char_2_text` | 0.115 | 0.8 |
| `char_3_text` | 0.129 | 0.8 |

All three templates miss → `in_team()` is always False → `in_team_and_world()` is always False → `is_main()` is always False → `ensure_main()` inevitably times out after 600 seconds (matching the `wait main` of exactly 601 seconds in the log). A scan of all 301 templates with region constraints and full-image search confirmed **no existing template can be reused** — the best match in the Android open world was 0.615 — so the decision has to be made in code.

**New `src/task/android_world.py` (Android-specific open-world detection)** — based on HUD elements unrelated to characters or scenes, with every threshold calibrated from real frames:

| Signal | Open world (measured) | Loading screen | Activity page | Login page | All-black frame | Threshold |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `radar_yellow` (yellow player arrow top-left radar) | 339 / 347 | 0 | 29 | 0 | 0 | ≥120 |
| `ult_orange` (gold concerto orb bottom-left) | 534 / 717 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | ≥300 |
| `party_white` (white bar in the top-right party bar) | 815 / 1057 | 215 | 619 | 208 | 0 | ≥600 |
| `hp_bar_white` (bottom HP bar) | 3023 / 2877 | 2240 | 0 | 1073 | 0 | ≥2200 |
| `loading_bar` (longest run of white pixels at the bottom) | 93 / 94 | **1313** | 381 | 74 | 0 | ≥800 means "loading" |

Decision rules: an all-black frame is judged false and logged separately; `loading_bar ≥ 800` is false (loading screen); `radar_yellow ≥ 120` or `ult_orange ≥ 300` is true; the fallback requires `party_white ≥ 600` **and** `hp_bar_white ≥ 2200` simultaneously.

> **The trap we hit**: judging the HP bar by "total white pixel count" made the **loading screen look like the open world** (loading progress bar 2240 ≥ 2200). A row-by-row analysis showed the loading progress bar is a **single row at y=1021~1027 spanning the whole screen (a continuous run of 1299 px)**, while the open-world HP bar is **two 85 px short segments** separated by tick marks. Switching to "longest continuous white run per row" separates them completely (1313 vs 93); the threshold 800 sits between the activity page (381) and the loading page (1313), with more than 2× margin on both sides.

**`src/task/BaseWWTask.py` changes**: `in_team()` starts with `if self.is_adb(): return self.in_team_android()`, leaving the PC branch untouched; the new `in_team_android()` returns an isomorphic triple; the PC branch used to be **completely silent** when all three templates missed, and now prints each template's actual confidence.

### MuMu capture black-frame interception (`ok/device/capture_methods/nemu_ipc.py`)

- Background: the MuMu IPC channel occasionally "returns success but with an all-black frame" (measured `mean=0`, `max=0`, 0% non-zero pixels). The upper layers treat a black frame as a normal frame, so no template matches — which looks exactly like "not in the open world".
- Handling: on a black frame the IPC connection is **rebuilt and retried 3 times** (0.5 s apart); if it still fails, a `CaptureException` with a clear Chinese reason is raised. The bare `Exception` (with no message) that the lower-level `@retry` throws when it gives up is wrapped with a readable explanation such as "please make sure the emulator is running, the window is visible, and background keep-alive is off".
- Detection threshold: a whole frame with `max ≤ 8` counts as empty (a normal frame with even a single non-black pixel is never misjudged).

### New Android "connection lost" dialog handling

- Scenario: after hanging in MuMu for a long time or during network jitter, the game shows the 【connection lost】 dialog. **The open world is still behind the dialog**, so `in_team` is still True and the task believes everything is fine — but every click is blocked by the dialog, which matches the reported "stuck and doing nothing" exactly.
- Handling (`BaseWWTask.handle_android_disconnect()`, executed in `is_main()` **before** the open-world check):
  1. Cheap brightness pre-filter: only continue when bright pixels (grayscale > 200) in the central region cover ≥ 0.5 of it (measured: 0.777 for the dialog, ≤ 0.101 for everything else), so OCR does not run on every `is_main` call;
  2. OCR confirms the text matches `连接已断开|断开连接|重新登录` (measured confidence 1.0);
  3. Click the 「确认」 button at the bottom of the dialog, reset `logged_in = False`, and let the existing `wait_login` log in again.
- PC / cloud mode are entirely unaffected (`is_adb()` is false, so it returns immediately).

### Auto-discovery of the MuMu bridge-mode ADB device

- The bridge-mode ADB port of MuMu is not fixed and often is not in the default port list; a new ARP-scan fallback discovers the device automatically (`ok/device/DeviceManager.py`).
- ARP output is decoded with the GBK locale in mind (`errors=replace`) so it does not blow up on a Chinese Windows.

### Android F1/F2 now tap the top-right icons directly

- **Requirement**: the user reported that "F2 打开任务环节也有问题，任务在右上角图标里面" and pointed out that **the icon to the left of it is the activity**, asking for both F2 and F1 to be changed to direct clicks on Android instead of key presses.
- **Touch devices have no F1/F2**: on PC, `F2` opens the Solla Guidebook and `F1` opens the activity menu; the Android touch client has neither, and sending `KEYCODE_F2` does nothing in the game. The old `openF2Book` branch was `if self.in_team_and_world() and self.touch.open_book(): ... else: send_key('f2')` — on Android, if it ever failed to take the icon-click branch it fell through to the key-press branch, waited out `wait_book`, and finally threw "can't find gray_book_boss, make sure f2 is the hotkey for book".

**New `src/task/android_book.py` (Android Solla Guidebook category icon detection)**:

The Android guidebook uses a mobile layout (a vertical column of category icons on the left plus a list on the right), and the icons differ in size and position from the PC templates. **Matching the PC templates directly with NCC hits the blank background** — measured on a real Android guidebook screen:

| Template | Best NCC score | Hit position | Actual icon position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gray_book_quest` | 0.712 | (48,156) | y≈191 |
| `gray_book_boss` | 0.741 | (44,283) | y≈322 |
| `gray_book_weekly` | 0.767 | (42,426) | y≈461 |
| `gray_book_all_monsters` | 0.822 | (39,695) | y≈727 |

Every best score lands on **the blank background at the template's own anchor** rather than on an icon — the detection fails and the click lands in the wrong place.

The replacement is a "**white-stroke binary mask + multi-scale IoU sliding window**": the narrow strip of icons on the left of the screen is binarized into a stroke mask → the PC templates are binarized the same way and cropped to their stroke bounding box → for 8 scale factors from 0.40 to 1.10 the exact per-position IoU is computed with an integral image and the global best is taken.

Key pitfalls:
- **The lower vertical bound must be clamped to 73% of the screen height.** The background gradient at the bottom brightens into a solid white area; after binarization the whole block becomes 1s, and any template can score a **false match of IoU=1.0** there (measured: `gray_book_all_monsters` hit at y=844/1038).
- **The upper vertical bound must exclude the title bar** (above 12% of screen height), otherwise the "materials" icon in the title is mistaken for a category icon.
- The screen and the template need **different mask flavours**: the screen uses a stroke mask (`S<110 and V>150`), the template is tried with both a grayscale mask and a stroke mask, and the higher score wins.

Measured on a real Android guidebook screen:

| PC template | Category | Android hit | IoU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gray_book_quest` | Quest (diamond star) | (76,191) | 0.78 |
| `gray_book_boss` | Boss (crossed swords) | (77,322) | 0.86 |
| `gray_book_weekly` | Weekly (loop badge) | (76,461) | 0.88 |
| `gray_book_all_monsters` | All monsters (skull mask) | (80,727) | 0.90 |

**`src/task/BaseWWTask.py` changes**: on Android, `openF2Book()` **only clicks** — the `send_key('f2')` fallback is removed entirely, while the PC branch is untouched; the new `open_touch_book()` taps the icon and then uses `is_book_opened()` to confirm the book really opened (tapping the icon again while the book is already open would close it), retrying once for up to 12 seconds; the new `touch_book_opened()`; the new `find_android_book_tab()` / `get_feature_template()`; the new `open_activity()` (the Android replacement for F1); `wait_book()` uses `find_android_book_tab` on Android while PC keeps `find_one` + `box_gray_book`.

**`src/task/touch_mode.py`** gained these coordinate constants:

| Constant | Meaning | Normalized coordinates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TOUCH_BOOK_CLICK` | F2 Solla Guidebook → the top-right **square book** icon (the 2nd one) | (0.7293, 0.0612) |
| `TOUCH_ACTIVITY_CLICK` | F1 Activity → the top-right **activity** icon (the 1st one) | (0.6813, 0.0602) |
